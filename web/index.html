03 / READ THE RESULTS HONESTLY

Useful experiment.
Early evidence.

This is an educational prototype, not an interpreter or accessibility service. The incomplete video mirror and weak hand tracking limit performance. Browser-upload accuracy has not yet been measured. It must not be relied on for communication, medical, legal, or safety decisions.
